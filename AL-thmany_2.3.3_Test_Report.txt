Implemented:
- versionCode 233 / versionName 2.3.3.
- Three safe Accessibility settings routes:

Important platform limitation:
- Android does not allow a normal app to enable its own AccessibilityService silently.
- DevicePolicyManager.getPermittedAccessibilityServices is restricted to device/profile owners.
  Therefore BLOCKED_BY_POLICY is reported only when that policy is actually readable.
